Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the noodles and meat.
  2. Cook the broth with ginger and onions.
  3. Cook the meat until it is ready.
  4. Put the noodles into a bowl.
  5. Add the meat and hot broth.
  6. Serve with fresh herbs and enjoy!

Making Pho takes time, but the result is delicious.
