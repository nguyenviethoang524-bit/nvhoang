Main Ingredients

These are the main ingredients of Pho:

  • Rice noodles
  • Beef or chicken
  • Beef broth or chicken broth
  • Ginger
  • Onions
  • Fresh herbs
  • Green onions

These ingredients make Pho delicious and full of flavor.
